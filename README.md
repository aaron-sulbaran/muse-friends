# Muse Friends

> Working name. Built at HackTX 2026 (Oct 24 to 25, 2026) for the Meta track, "Bringing People Closer Together with AI."

Your agent and your friend's agent notice the overlap (you both keep sending shawarma reels, you are both free Tuesday afternoon) and each asks its human one question: *want to reach out?* Say yes and your agent drafts the message, then hands you off to your real messaging app to send it yourself. Agents do the coordinating; people do the talking.

We built it the way the Muse team would: as a feature inside Muse, Meta's personal agent, with both agents running on Muse Spark through the Meta Model API. It's a concept build for a hackathon, not a shipped Meta product.
